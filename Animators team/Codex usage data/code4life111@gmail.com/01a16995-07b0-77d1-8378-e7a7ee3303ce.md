    The most useful applications would be:
    
    1. **Audio controls.** Gain, fades, slip and locking in one compact box. Numeric fields provide precision; sliders and steppers provide quick adjustments.     Two connector meanings must remain visually distinct:
    
    - **Item → box:** a quiet tether explaining which item owns the controls.

    I would make the boxes behave consistently:

    - Dragging the header moves only the box. Outside clicks leave it open; Close dismisses it.
