## Assistant (started 2026-10-04T11:40:42.628Z)

    Yes—that’s the architecture I’d recommend for your **300-aircraft target**.
    
    | Trail portion | Proposed renderer |
    |---|---|
    | Fresh wake near the aircraft | Niagara or localized volumes for distinct engine trails and fine breakup |
    | Transition region | Both systems share trajectory, width, age and noise, with complementary density weights |
    | Broad, older wake farther behind | A contrail density branch inside YH_Sky’s existing cloud material |
    
    **Both systems should reconstruct from the same flight history.** That gives a complete long trail at frame zero without simulating hundreds of earlier frames. Renderer history may still need some initialization.
    
    Place the handover using **trail age or distance behind the aircraft**, with camera distance controlling detail quality. That keeps the transition stable during camera movement. We can also promote an older trail segment if the camera approaches it.
    
    The main challenge is matching the two appearances: an opacity crossfade alone can produce a bright seam or double-darkening. We need matching shape and density, then tests under backlighting, cloud crossings and motion blur. Sharing the data makes that manageable, but it still needs visual validation.
    
    This also reduces pressure on the cloud renderer: it handles the broader wake while the separate system supplies the finest detail. YH_Sky still needs the density input and an extension that separates the weather deck’s height from the full cloud-rendering bounds.
