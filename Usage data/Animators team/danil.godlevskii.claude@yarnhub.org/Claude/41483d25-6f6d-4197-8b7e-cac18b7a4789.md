    **Review:** a separate reviewer checked the whole build and found five real problems, all fixed and tested (54 tests passing):
    - **The serious one:** the effects check only looked one level deep, so `NS_Me262_Guns` passed as clean while its spark texture was missing. It now walks the full dependency tree, and all 50 missing assets are traced to the effects that use them.
    - **README counts:** it claimed more assets were copied than actually were.
    - **Half-written files:** a crash could leave a half-written FBX recorded as good. Files are now written under a temporary name and renamed only once complete.
    - **No way to redo work:** the force and retry switches above were missing.
    - **Hangs:** a hung editor would have stalled the run forever. It's now killed after 10 minutes without progress.
